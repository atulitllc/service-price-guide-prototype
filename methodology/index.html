Methodology: How We Estimate Prices

Last checked Oct 13, 2026

We publish only figures we can cite, each with a link and a check date.

Sources

  • US Bureau of Labor Statistics, OEWS (May 2025 data). Hourly wage 10th percentile, median and 90th percentile, nationally and for the Boston, Austin and Denver metro areas, pulled from the free BLS public API. These are worker wages, not customer prices. bls.gov/oes
  • HomeAdvisor cost guides for plumbers, electricians, HVAC repair, house cleaning and handyman work. National ranges only, labeled Estimate, with attribution. plumbers electricians hvac house-cleaners handyman

Index rule

A city page needs at least 3 real local data points before it can be indexed. Today every city page has 3 local BLS wage points. The whole prototype stays noindex.

Gaps

We have no free, citable local job price data for any city, and no national job cost guide yet for movers, painters or pet sitters.

Local service price guides. Funded by matched quote requests and sponsored partner links, never by what a price says.
