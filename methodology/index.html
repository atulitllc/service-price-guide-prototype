Methodology: How We Estimate Prices

Last updated Oct 13, 2026

Every price on CityRateBook is a range labeled Estimate. In this prototype all figures are sample data.

Method

  1. Collect local data points: contractor rate cards, permit fee schedules, homeowner surveys.
  2. Require at least 3 local data points and 2 city specific factors before a city page can be indexed. Pages that fail are set to noindex, follow.
  3. Trim outliers and report the middle 80% as the typical range, with the median.
  4. Recheck every 90 days and show the last checked date next to each price.
