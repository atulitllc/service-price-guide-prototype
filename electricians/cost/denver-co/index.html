Local cost factors in Denver

1

Labor: Rates sit above national averages, about $85 to $140 per hour for licensed trades.

2

Permits: Colorado licenses plumbers and electricians through DORA; Denver Community Planning and Development requires permits for most mechanical work.

3

Seasonal demand: Cold snaps lift furnace and pipe calls in winter, while spring hail season ties up roofers and handymen.

4

Housing stock: altitude adjusted furnace sizing and 1950s ranch homes can add time.
