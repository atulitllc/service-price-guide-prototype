Privacy policy

Last updated Oct 13, 2026 · prototype draft

What we collect

When you request quotes: service, ZIP, timing, job details, name, email and optional phone.

Who we share it with

Up to 3 matched local service providers, only after you check the consent box.

Calls and texts

If you give a phone number and consent, matched providers may call or text you, including by automated means. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to opt out.

This prototype

Nothing is sent. Requests are stored in your browser's localStorage and you can clear them anytime.

Local service price guides. Funded by matched quote requests and sponsored partner links, never by what a price says.
