Local cost factors in Boston

1

Labor: Union scale and Massachusetts licensing keep hourly trade rates high, often $95 to $160 for licensed work.

2

Permits: Massachusetts requires licensed plumbers and electricians for most work, and Boston Inspectional Services issues permits for water heaters, panels, and new circuits.

3

Seasonal demand: Winter brings heating calls and frozen pipe emergencies; September 1 lease turnover drives moving and cleaning demand.

4

Housing stock: older triple deckers with tight access and knob and tube wiring can add time.
