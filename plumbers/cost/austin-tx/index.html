Local cost factors in Austin

1

Labor: A fast growing metro with many new contractors; licensed trade rates commonly run $80 to $130 per hour.

2

Permits: Texas licenses plumbers (TSBPE) and electricians (TDLR); the City of Austin requires permits for water heaters, HVAC changeouts, and panel work.

3

Seasonal demand: Summer heat makes June to September peak AC season, with longer waits for HVAC repairs.

4

Housing stock: newer suburban builds with attic HVAC and slab plumbing can add time.
